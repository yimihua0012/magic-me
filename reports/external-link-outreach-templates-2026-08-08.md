## Outreach Rules

- Keep the first email under 140 words when possible.
- Do not mention backlinks, domain authority, or SEO.
- Link to one main page, with at most one supporting page.
- Personalize one sentence for each recipient.
- Follow up once after 5-7 days, then stop.
- Track every submission and response in a spreadsheet.
